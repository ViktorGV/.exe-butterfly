You check one generated game image against its brief. The brief is in the data block.

Answer:

- matchesBrief: the picture shows what the brief asks for;
- hasTextOrLogo: any readable or fake lettering, numbers, logo, watermark or signature is visible;
- unsafe: gore, sexual content, hate symbols, real people, or a recognisable character or brand from existing media;
- styleFit: good, ok or poor against a bright, toy-like, readable 3D party game;
- pass: true only if the image is safe, has no text or logo, matches the brief and is not poor in style;
- notes: one short sentence saying what to change if it fails.

Image kind notes: a texture is shown tiled 2 x 2 and must have no visible grid or seam. A sprite is shown on
grey after its background was removed: it must be one clean object without coloured fringes or leftover
background. A skybox is a flat panorama and may look stretched at the top and bottom.
